# Virginia Velocity Game Day — Club Director User Guide

A practical guide for directors and coaches who run the club from their phone or tablet on the sideline.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Virginia Velocity Game Day** helps your club run seasons, teams, and match days in one place — and turn what happens on the field into clear records of player development.

**What it is for**

- **Club leadership** — Set up seasons and teams, build age-group player pools, invite staff, and open a full picture of any player’s growth over time.
- **Game day** — Build lineups, manage the live match (subs, goals, positions), and share a simple sideline link so an assistant can log micro-stats without touching the coach’s phone.
- **After the whistle** — Capture honest staff notes for yourselves, rate performances, and draft a parent-friendly weekly recap email that shares minutes and positions — without exposing internal ratings or critical tags.
- **Season insight** — Review finished matches, playing time, scoring, and development trends so roster and training decisions are grounded in evidence, not memory.

**What it is not**

- It is **not** a parent portal. Parents do not log in. The only parent-facing output is the recap email you choose to send.
- It does **not** replace your registrar or Sprocket as a system of record for club registration — it works alongside them (including roster CSV import).

**Who can do what**

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Director** | Everything below, plus **Club Admin** (seasons, teams, staff, club-wide player directory). |
| **Staff** (Head / Assistant Coach) | Run game day for their assigned teams, recaps, reporting, and team management (with some actions limited to Head Coach / Director). |

Sign-in is by **magic link** (email) — no passwords to manage.

## 2. Director Workflows

Open **Club Admin** from the menu (Directors only). You will see three tabs: **Club Setup**, **Staff**, and **Players**.

### 2.1 Manage seasons

Seasons keep rosters, matches, and stats organized. Only **one season is Active** at a time — that season is what coaches use for rosters and games.

1. Go to **Club Admin → Club Setup → Season Manager**.
2. Create a season with a clear name (for example, *Fall 2026*) and optional start/end months.
3. Tap **Set Active** on the season you want the club to use now.
4. When a season ends, **Archive** it. History and stats stay available; it simply stops being the default for new games.

**Why it matters:** An active season is required before you can assign players from the pool to teams and before coaches can properly attach matches to the current campaign.

### 2.2 Create and manage teams

Still under **Club Setup → Teams**:

1. Choose an **age group** (this also sets the lineup format, such as 7v7 vs 9v9).
2. Enter the team nickname and **Create Team**. Names appear with the age prefix everywhere (for example, **U11 Capybaras**).
3. Edit age group or name anytime. **Archive** a team when it will not return next season — match history remains intact. **Restore** if you need it again.

### 2.3 Build the age-group player pool

Players live in an **Age-Group Player Pool** first, then get assigned to a team for the active season. This is how you form teams without losing club-wide player identity.

1. Scroll to **Age-Group Player Pool** (near the bottom of Club Setup).
2. Select the **age group**.
3. Add players manually (**First**, **Last**, **#** → **Add to Pool**), or use **Import from Sprocket** to upload a roster CSV (name, jersey, position).
4. Choose a team under **Assign selected players to team (active season)** and tap **Assign** for each unassigned player.
5. By default you only see players **not yet on a team** — ideal while you are still forming squads. Use **Show players already on a team** if you need to review everyone. **Archive** players who leave so they drop out of selectors while their history remains.

**Tip:** Activate a season before you rely on the unassigned list. Without an active season, the app will not treat players as free to assign.

### 2.4 View the Player Directory and development report (“dossier”)

1. Open **Club Admin → Players → Player Directory**.
2. Search by name, jersey, or team. Filter by **age group** and **team** (including **Unassigned**). Choosing an age group narrows the team list to teams in that age group only.
3. Tap a player to open their **development report** — a dossier-style view of how they have grown.

On the report you can switch between **Career / Overall** and the **active season**, and review:

- **Cumulative analytics** — matches, minutes, goals, assists, key defensive and creative actions, and plus/minus over time  
- **Team & roster history** — where they have been assigned  
- **Guest play history** — when they appeared as a guest for another squad  

**Why it matters:** This is the director’s lens on player development across the club — not just one coach’s notebook after one game.

### 2.5 Invite and manage staff

Under **Club Admin → Staff**:

1. Review the **Staff Roster** (Directors and Staff, with per-team coaching roles).
2. **Create Staff Account** — enter email and display name, choose **Director** or **Staff**, assign teams as **Head Coach** or **Assistant Coach**, then send the invite. They receive a magic link that sets up their access.
3. Manage **Pending Invites**, change roles later under **Manage Access**, or **Revoke Access** when someone leaves.

People who create an account without an invite land in **Access Pending** until a Director assigns them.

## 3. Coach Workflows

Coaches (and Directors acting as coaches) start from **Home**: select the team, then use **New Game**, **Team Management**, **View Recaps**, or **Reporting**.

### 3.1 Manage game day

**Before kickoff — Game Day Setup**

1. From Home, tap **New Game**.
2. Confirm **head coach**, **opponent**, **date**, **time**, **home/away**, tournament flag if needed, and **half length**.
3. Mark who is attending and build the **1st half lineup** on the pitch (or load a saved **lineup preset** from Team Management).
4. Add missing players from the age-group pool or create a new player. Mark **Is Guest Player?** when someone is borrowing for the day.
5. Tap **Ready for 1st Half**, then **Start 1st Half** when you are live.

**During the match**

- Manage the live pitch: substitutions, position changes, and formation.
- Log **Goal** and **Opp. Goal**.
- **End 1st Half** → adjust **Halftime Setup** / 2nd half lineup → **Start 2nd Half**.
- When finished, **End of Game** (note whether you finished on time or went to added time). You will move into **Post-Game Recap**.

**Note:** The team selector stays locked while a match is live so you do not accidentally switch squads mid-game.

### 3.2 Add guest players

Guests keep the match fair when you are short without polluting “true” season roster identity.

- On **Game Day Setup**, add from the age pool or create a player with **Is Guest Player?** on.
- In **Team Management → Roster**, you can also mark **Guest player** when adding someone for that team context.
- Guest appearances show up later on the club development report under **Guest Play History**, so directors still see the full picture.

### 3.3 Log sideline stats

For busy touchlines, hand stats to a parent or assistant:

1. During the live match, tap **Share Stat Tracker** and send or copy the link.
2. On that link, they use **Sideline Stat Tracker** — tap an action (goal, shot, assist, tackle, and similar), then the player. Built for fast outdoor tapping.
3. Events flow back into the coach’s **Sideline Stats** panel and feed post-game review and season reporting.

The link is for **that match only**. When the game ends, sideline logging stops.

### 3.4 Generate weekly parent recaps

After the match (or from a finished match in **View Recaps**), open **Post-Game Recap**:

1. Fill **Internal Coach Notes** for staff eyes only — tactics, critical feedback, what to work on in training.
2. Complete **Player Review** ratings and notes for development tracking.
3. Tap **Generate Parent Recap Email**.

In the **Parent Recap** modal:

- Write (or edit) the **parent-facing game summary** — balanced and constructive.
- Optionally add a **Focus for next week** (practice theme).
- Review the email preview: subject line, game summary, **player minutes & positions** only (for example, *Alex: 45 mins (CB, CDM)*), and next-week focus.
- **Save summary**, **Copy to Clipboard**, or **Send via Email**.

**Important:** Internal ratings, plus/minus, and developmental critique **do not** go into the parent email. That keeps communication warm for families while coaches still get an honest staff record.

If your club has AI drafting configured, you may also see **Draft with AI**, which rewrites internal notes into a parent-safe summary for you to review before sending.

Finish with **Finalize & Save** when the staff recap is complete. Home will remind you with **Complete Match Recap** / **Game Recap Needed** if anything is still open.

### 3.5 Review history and season reporting

- **View Recaps** — open any finished match to revisit notes, ratings, sideline stats, and (when saved) the parent-facing summary.
- **Reporting** — season record, previous matches, player breakdowns, playing time, scoring & defense, player development trends, and more.

Use these views in staff meetings when discussing who needs more minutes, who is ready for a new role, or how a team is trending across the season.

## Quick tips for directors

| Tip | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| **Start with season → teams → pool → staff** | That order unblocks coaches cleanly. |
| **Sunlight mode** | Use the **Sunlight** toggle for high-contrast outdoor readability after the final whistle. |
| **Archive, don’t delete** | Archiving seasons, teams, or players hides them from day-to-day selectors but keeps history for development reports. |
| **Pending access** | If someone cannot get in, check **Club Admin → Staff** and assign a role and teams. |
| **No active season?** | Create and activate one in Club Admin before assigning pool players or expecting season-tied rosters. |
